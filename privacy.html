Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how StudyForge protects your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use StudyForge, we collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Email address and account credentials for secure access
  • Payment information (processed securely by Flutterwave, PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Lecture notes, PDFs, and content you upload
  • Usage data and preferences to improve your experience

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide and improve StudyForge's features:

  • Provide and improve our AI study guide generator
  • Process your subscriptions and payments securely
  • Communicate with you about your account and progress
  • Analyze usage to enhance our AI features
  • Protect against unauthorized access

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including 256-bit encryption, secure servers, and regular security audits. Your payment information is never stored on our servers – it's processed directly by Flutterwave, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor.
