10 Study Tips That Actually Work (Backed by Science)

Not all study methods are created equal. Here are 10 scientifically proven techniques that will help you learn faster, retain more, and ace your exams.

1. Active Recall

The most effective learning technique. Instead of re-reading notes, test yourself. Close the book and try to recall the information. Studies show active recall improves retention by 50%.

2. Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks. This moves information from short-term to long-term memory. Apps like StudyForge make this automatic.

3. Pomodoro Technique

Study in 25-minute focused sprints, then take a 5-minute break. After 4 sessions, take a longer 15-minute break. This prevents burnout and maintains focus.

4. Teach Someone Else

If you can explain a concept simply, you truly understand it. Study groups or even explaining to an imaginary student works.

5. Practice Testing

Take practice exams. This is one of the strongest predictors of exam performance. Use quiz generators to create unlimited practice questions.

6. Interleaving

Mix different subjects or topics in one study session. This helps your brain distinguish between concepts and improves problem-solving.

7. Elaborative Interrogation

Ask "why" questions constantly. "Why does this work? Why is this important?" This deepens understanding.

8. Self-Explanation

Explain how you solved a problem or understood a concept. Verbalizing your thought process reinforces learning.

9. Dual Coding

Combine words with visuals. Diagrams, mind maps, and charts help create stronger memory connections.

10. Sleep & Exercise

Your brain consolidates memories while you sleep. 7-9 hours of sleep improves recall by 20-40%. Exercise increases blood flow to the brain.
