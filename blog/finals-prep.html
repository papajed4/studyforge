How to Prepare for Finals in 2 Weeks

Two weeks until finals. Panic setting in? Don't worry. Here's a proven 14-day plan to maximize your study time and walk into that exam room confident.

Week 1: Foundation & Organization

Days 1-2: Gather all your materials. Organize notes by topic. Use StudyForge to generate summaries and flashcards for each chapter.

Days 3-5: Focus on understanding core concepts. Use active recall - test yourself on each topic daily.

Days 6-7: Create practice questions. Identify weak areas and review them.

Week 2: Intense Review & Practice

Days 8-10: Take full practice exams. Simulate real exam conditions. Review every mistake thoroughly.

Days 11-12: Focus on weak topics identified from practice exams. Use flashcards for active recall.

Day 13: Light review. Go over key concepts and formulas. Get good sleep.

Day 14: Exam day. Trust your preparation. Stay calm.

💡 Pro Tip

Use StudyForge's exam mode to generate practice questions. The AI analyzes your notes and predicts likely exam topics.

Daily Schedule (4-6 hours/day)

  • Morning (2 hours): Active recall with flashcards
  • Break: 15 min walk or stretch
  • Mid-day (2 hours): Practice questions and weak topics
  • Afternoon (1 hour): Review and summarize
  • Evening: Light review, 30 min Pomodoro sessions
